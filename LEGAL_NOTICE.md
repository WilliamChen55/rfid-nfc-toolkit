# Legal Notice / Acceptable Use

This repository is published for **legitimate hardware development, engineering diagnostics, education and authorized security research**.

## Permitted use

- Reading and identifying tags, cards, fobs and labels that **you own**, or that you have **explicit written authorization** to test.
- Development and debugging of your own readers, firmware and integrations.
- Protocol education, coursework and lab exercises on equipment you control.
- Enterprise asset-management, inventory and access-system acceptance testing performed by or on behalf of the system owner.

## Not permitted

- Accessing, cloning or emulating credentials, badges, payment cards or access tokens you do not own or are not authorized to test.
- Bypassing, defeating or degrading access-control, payment or transit systems.
- Building, selling or distributing "universal" credential or access-bypass tools.
- Any activity that violates radio regulations, computer-misuse law, or the terms of a system you do not own.

This project deliberately **does not include** key-recovery, credential-recovery, encryption-bypass or duplication functionality.

## Regulatory notes

- Radio transmission equipment (including SDR hardware and readers) is regulated. In the United States, transmitters must comply with FCC rules (47 CFR Part 15/Part 90 as applicable); in the EU, with the Radio Equipment Directive (2014/53/EU) and CE marking. **Deliberate interference and jamming are prohibited in essentially every jurisdiction.**
- Sale and use of reading/writing hardware may require type approval, import registration or a dealer licence in your market. Check before shipping.
- Nothing in this repository constitutes legal advice.
